Refund Policy

1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work on the GIG Seva platform. GIG Seva is a marketplace connecting Workers, Contractors, and Business Owners. We act only as an intermediary and do not directly provide services.

2. General Policy

All transactions between users are handled directly between them. GIG Seva does not guarantee refunds for any service or work completed between users.

3. No Automatic Refunds

GIG Seva does not provide automatic refunds for:

  • Completed work or services
  • Chat-based agreements between users
  • Direct payments made outside the platform

4. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered only in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment (if platform payment system is used)
  • Technical error in payment processing
  • Unauthorized transaction (verified case)

5. User-to-User Payments

GIG Seva is not responsible for payments made between Workers, Contractors, and Business Owners. Any refund or dispute must be resolved between the involved users.

6. Dispute Resolution

In case of disputes between users, GIG Seva may assist in communication but is not legally responsible for resolving payment conflicts or guaranteeing refunds.

7. Work Cancellation

If a job or project is cancelled before completion, refund terms depend on mutual agreement between users. GIG Seva does not enforce refund decisions.

8. Fraud or Abuse Cases

If fraud, scam, or misuse is detected, GIG Seva may:

  • Suspend user accounts
  • Block transactions
  • Assist authorities if required

9. Platform Fees (If Applicable)

If GIG Seva introduces service fees in future, those fees will be non-refundable unless stated otherwise.

10. Payment Gateway Refunds

If payments are processed through third-party gateways, refunds will follow the policies of that provider.

11. Changes to Refund Policy

GIG Seva reserves the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform means acceptance of changes.
