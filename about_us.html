About Us

1. Who We Are

GIG Seva is a digital platform designed to connect Workers, Contractors, and Business Owners in one place. Our goal is to simplify how people find work, manage projects, and build professional connections.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is to create a reliable and accessible platform where individuals can find work opportunities, communicate easily, and grow their professional network without barriers.

3. What We Do

  • Provide a platform for job connections
  • Enable real-time chat between users
  • Support Workers in finding work opportunities
  • Help Contractors manage and assign work
  • Allow Business Owners to hire and manage projects

4. Our Platform Roles

4.1 Workers

Workers can create profiles, offer services, and connect with Contractors or Business Owners to get work opportunities.

4.2 Contractors

Contractors can assign, manage, and outsource work efficiently through the platform.

4.3 Business Owners

Business Owners can post jobs, hire skilled Workers, and manage their projects seamlessly.

5. Why Choose GIG Seva

  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Fast communication with built-in chat system
  • Flexible work opportunities
  • Secure and growing platform

6. Our Vision

We aim to become a trusted platform across India where people can connect, collaborate, and grow professionally using technology.

7. Disclaimer

GIG Seva acts only as an intermediary platform. We do not guarantee job availability, income, or outcomes. All agreements and transactions are between users.
